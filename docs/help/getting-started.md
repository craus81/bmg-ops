# Getting started

Each section is one task.

---

## Sign in

1. Go to the BMG Fleet URL.
2. Type your work email.
3. Tap **Send Magic Link**.
4. Check your email and tap the link in the message titled "Sign in
   to BMG Fleet".

You're in. Bookmark the page for next time.

Magic links expire after about an hour. If yours expired, request a
new one.

---

## Set up two-step sign-in

FleetSuite can ask for a six-digit code from an app on your phone each
time you sign in, so your password alone can't get anyone in. Everyone who
works with BMG's money (super admin, executive, finance, bookkeeper, CPA)
needs it once a super admin switches it on.

1. Put an authenticator app on your phone: the iPhone's **Passwords** app,
   **Google Authenticator**, **Microsoft Authenticator** or **1Password**.
2. In FleetSuite, tap your avatar → **Settings** → **Two-step sign-in** →
   **Set up two-step sign-in**.
3. Scan the QR code with the app. On the same phone, tap **Add it to your
   authenticator app** instead, or copy the key into the app.
4. Type the six-digit code the app shows for **BMG FleetSuite**.
5. Tap **Save and carry on**.

From then on, each time you sign in on a device, FleetSuite asks for the
code the app shows. The super admins hear that you set it up, so one set up
by someone else is caught.

---

## Move two-step sign-in to a new phone

1. **Settings** → **Two-step sign-in** → **Move to a new phone**.
2. Scan the QR code with the authenticator app on the new phone.
3. Type its code and tap **Save and carry on**.

The old phone's codes stop working.

---

## I lost my phone and can't give the code

Ask a super admin to reset your two-step sign-in. You're signed out
everywhere; sign in with your password and set it up again.

---

## I just signed up and my account says "Pending"

An admin needs to approve you. Ping your shop's admin and ask them to
approve your account at **Admin → Users**.

---

## Install on your phone (iPhone)

1. Open the BMG Fleet URL in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm.

The icon appears on your home screen and runs full-screen.

---

## Install on your phone (Android)

1. Open the BMG Fleet URL in Chrome.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen** or **Install app**.
4. Confirm.

---

## Edit my profile

1. Tap your avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Fill in name, phone, any role-specific fields.
4. Tap **Save**.

---

## Turn notifications on or off

1. Tap **Settings**.
2. Toggle the rows you want.
3. Save.

---

## Switch your view to test what someone else sees (admins only)

1. Tap your avatar.
2. Tap **View as…** and pick a role.
3. The header shows a yellow banner: "Viewing as: <role>".
4. Tap **Exit View As** to return.

---

## Sign out

1. Tap your avatar.
2. Tap **Sign Out**.

---

## Get help

- Tap the **AI** button (floating mascot, bottom-right) to ask
  FleetSuite AI. It can answer most questions and pulls from this help
  library. Tell it about a problem or an idea, or ask something it can't
  answer, and it sends it to Craig as a request for you (More →
  Requests) and tells you the request number.
- Tap **Admin → Knowledge** if you're an admin to browse the full
  library.
- Or ping your shop's admin.

---

## Report a problem or ask for a feature

1. Tap **More → Requests** (it's at the top) and tap **+ New request**, or use the Requests card at the bottom of Home.
2. Pick **Problem**, **Idea** or **Question** and say what happened in plain words.
3. Add a screenshot if it helps: drag it in, paste it, or tap upload. FleetSuite notes which screen you came from.
4. Tap **Send request**.

Craig hears about it right away. FleetSuite AI reads new requests on a schedule and writes the answer under the request, and you get a bell when it does. The status shows where it stands: **New**, **Looking into it**, **Waiting on Craig**, **Done**.

Someone already asked for the same thing? Tap **Me too** on their request instead of sending a new one.

---

## Ask FleetSuite AI

Tap the round mascot button in the corner of any page. Ask in plain words, for example "What vehicles are in the shop right now?" or "How do I send an estimate for approval?". It reads FleetSuite's own data and these help guides.

It can also do things when you ask: make a draft estimate, start a graphics job, move a graphics job to a new status, or send someone a message.

If you tell it about a bug or an idea, or ask something it can't answer, it files a Request for you and tells you the request number. Craig hears about it right away.

---

## Find a page in More

Type in the search box at the top of **More** to jump to any page. Groups fold and unfold when you tap their names, and a folded group shows how many things are waiting in it. Tap **(i)** next to a page to read what it's for.

---

## Leave a page without saving

Long forms and transactions have one Save bar pinned at the top. **Save** keeps your changes. **Leave without saving** throws away what you changed and takes you back.
