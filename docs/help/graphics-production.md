# Graphics

Each section is one task. Read the one you need.

---

## See all graphics jobs

1. Tap **Graphics** in the bottom nav.
2. Each row is a job. The top of the page shows status counts:
   **Proofing**, **Production**, etc.
3. Tap a status chip to filter.

---

## Create a new graphics job

1. **Graphics** → tap **+ New Graphics Job**.
2. Pick a job type:
   - **Production** — full design → proof → print → install.
   - **Customer Supplied** — customer is providing the artwork.
   - **Proofing** — design and approval only, no production yet.
3. Pick the customer and vehicle(s).
4. Type a title.
5. Tap **Create**.

Most jobs auto-create from estimates — only build manually for
one-off work.

---

## Upload a working file or proof

1. Open the graphics job.
2. Tap **Upload File** in the **Proofs / Working Files** section.
3. Drag the file in.
4. Tap **Save**.

You can upload as many files as you want.

---

## Send a proof to the customer for approval

The customer should see **only one file** — not every working file.

1. Open the graphics job.
2. Tap **Send proof for customer approval**.
3. Pick **one** file from the list.
4. Pick **Email**, **SMS**, or both.
5. Tap **Send to customer**.

The customer gets a link, opens it on a public page, and approves or
requests revisions. You'll be notified either way.

---

## Resend the approval link

1. Open the job.
2. Tap **Resend approval link**.
3. Pick a channel.
4. Confirm.

---

## Move a job to "in production"

After the customer approves:

1. Open the job.
2. Tap the status chip.
3. Pick **Mark In Production**.

---

## Mark a job ready to pick up

When the work is done and waiting for the customer to come get it:

1. Open the job.
2. Tap the status chip.
3. Pick **Mark Ready to Pickup**.

The customer is auto-notified.

---

## Mark a job shipped

1. Open the job.
2. Tap the status chip.
3. Pick **Mark Shipped**.
4. (Optional) Type the tracking number.

The owner and accounting are notified to invoice.

---

## Create an invoice for a shipped graphics job

After marking shipped, you (or whoever invoices) get a bell
notification with a **Create Invoice** action.

1. Tap the bell notification, or open the job and tap **Create
   Invoice**.
2. Pick the customer.
3. Review the line items, qty, and rate.
4. Tap **Create Invoice**.

The NetSuite invoice number shows up on the job.

---

## Download every file on a job

1. Open the job.
2. Tap **Download All** in the **Proofs / Working Files** section.

A zip downloads with every file.

---

## Link a graphics job to its parent upfit project

Most jobs auto-link from their estimate. To link manually:

1. Open the graphics job.
2. Scroll to **Parent Upfit Project**.
3. Tap **Link to Project**.
4. Search by customer or VIN, pick the project.

---

## See only your assigned jobs

1. **Graphics** → filter chip **Mine**.
2. Or use the **My Jobs** widget on your home dashboard.

---

## Tell ops the graphics are ready for install

Move the job's production status to **Ready**. The system
automatically notifies the assigned installer and admins. You don't
need to do anything else.
