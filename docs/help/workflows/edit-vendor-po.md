# Edit a vendor PO

Change a purchase order that's already in NetSuite without leaving
FleetSuite. Anyone who can order parts can do this.

---

## Where it is

**More → Purchase Orders** (Purchasing → **Vendor POs** tab). Every open
PO has a **✎ Edit PO** button next to ✉ Email vendor.

A PO that's fully received or closed has no Edit PO button. Change those
in NetSuite.

---

## What you can change

- **Quantity** of any part.
- **Price** of any part. Leave the price box blank to keep NetSuite's.
- **Remove** a part.
- **Add a part.** Type the item number or name under the parts list.
- **Move** parts to another vendor. Tick **Move** on each part, then pick
  the vendor. Saving makes a new PO with that vendor for those parts and
  takes them off this one.
- **Expected date.** This is NetSuite's Receive By date. It also shows as
  the ETA on the job.
- **Ship to.** Tap **Change** to type an address, or **Ship to the shop**
  to put it back to O'Fallon.
- **Memo.**

Tap **Save to NetSuite**. The PO changes in NetSuite right away. **Leave
without saving** closes the window and changes nothing.

---

## What's locked

- **Received parts.** The quantity can't go below what already came in,
  and the part can't be removed or moved. A wrong part that was received
  is a vendor return in NetSuite.
- **Billed or closed lines** can't change at all. They show a 🔒.
- **The last part.** A PO always keeps at least one part. To cancel a
  whole PO, close it in NetSuite.

If someone changes the PO in NetSuite while you have the window open,
saving stops with a red message and nothing changes. Close the window
and open it again to see their changes.

---

## Parts for jobs

Each part shows the job it's for. When you lower a quantity a job needs,
or remove the part, the parts no longer on order go back to the **parts
queue** for that job, the same as Undo. Order them again from there.

Moved parts stay tied to their jobs on the new PO.

---

## Telling the vendor

FleetSuite never emails the vendor by itself.

If the PO was already emailed, the edit window shows a yellow note. After
you save, the green banner has **✉ Email the vendor the change**. It opens
the email window with the PO attached and a list of what changed, which
you can edit before you send. When parts moved, there's also a button to
email the new vendor their new PO.
