# Graphics → install handoff

The moves from "graphics is done with the design and production" to
"the vehicle is installed and out the door."

---

## The flow

1. **Graphics flips the job to Ready.**
   - Open the graphics job. Tap the status chip → **Mark Ready**.
2. **The right people get notified automatically.**
   - The assigned installer, admins, and anyone who's opted in.
3. **Installer schedules the install.**
   - Open **CNI Jobs** or **Ready for Install**, tap the row, tap
     **Schedule**, pick a slot.
4. **Vehicle arrives. Shop tech checks it in.**
   - Tap **Check In**, scan VIN, take photos, tap **Save**.
5. **Installer opens the pick-list and works.**
   - From the queue or by scanning the VIN.
6. **Installer marks complete.**
   - All required QC checked, completion photo uploaded, notes
     written. Tap **Mark Complete**.
7. **Customer is auto-notified.**

---

## Both lanes have to be green

A vehicle has two lanes: the upfit lane and the graphics-install
lane. Both must be done before the vehicle is fully complete. The
**In-Shop** page shows both.

If the upfit is done but graphics still isn't installed, the vehicle
stays open.

---

## What to do if graphics aren't ready when the installer is

1. Don't start the install.
2. Tap **💬 Message Customer** or open **Admin → Inbox** to ping
   graphics or sales.
3. Wait for graphics to flip the job to **Ready** before scheduling
   the install.
